# NOTE: "Cartão de visita" (Reel 4 of the 3D series, 12 s)

`final.mp4`: 1080x1920, 30 fps, no audio. Plan in `STORYBOARD.md`.

## Idea
CODE SITE's card falls onto a desk and flips to its QR. The camera dives into the QR until four modules fill the frame, then match-cuts on black-on-cream to the homepage hero type and dollies out to the phone, where the page scrolls.

## Real and illustrative
- **Real:**
  - The QR encodes https://codesite.online (generated with `qrcode`), so pointing a camera at the Reel opens the site.
  - The phone screen is a 2500 px capture of the homepage (local production build).
  - The mark and the number are CODE SITE's own.
- **Illustrative:** the card design is drawn for the film.
