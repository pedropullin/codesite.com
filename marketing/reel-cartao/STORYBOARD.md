# Reel 4: "Cartão de visita"

1080x1920, 30 fps, 12.0 s (360 f), clock smooth, smear on type only. No sound. Approved plan: the "Roteiros 3D CODE SITE" deck, Reel 4.

## PAGE
- **Problem:** a business card is read once and ends up in a drawer.
- **Audience:** small business owners on Instagram.
- **Goal:** they message CODE SITE on WhatsApp.
- **Emotion:** "my card should open my site".
- **The one true thing:** a site lives in the customer's pocket. The QR on the card in the film is real and opens codesite.online.

## Idea
CODE SITE's own business card falls onto a desk and flips to its QR. The camera dives into the QR until its modules are big black and cream squares. A match cut to the same black-on-cream of the homepage's hero type ("CÓDIGO QUE VIRA EXPERIÊNCIA.") is followed by a dolly out to the phone, where the page scrolls.

**Accent:** CODE SITE blue, on the slash of the mark and on "gaveta." in the CTA.

## World
- **Desk:** dark walnut under a warm desk lamp, with a cool rim.
- **Card:** thick black stock, the mark and name printed on the front, the QR on cream on the back.
- **Phone shot:** the same warm room, darker.
- **CTA:** paper.
- **Hero moment:** the dive through the QR.
